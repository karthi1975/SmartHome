# Smart Home Controller - Voice Command Guide

## Getting Started
The app starts with voice control already active - no wake word needed! Just speak naturally to control your smart home and access health information.

## Voice Command Flow Examples

### 1. Temperature Control Flow
**Scenario**: Kitchen is too hot and you want to lower the temperature

**You say**: "The kitchen is hot"
- App navigates to Kitchen page automatically
- Shows current temperature

**You say**: "Lower the temperature" or "Make it cooler"
- Temperature animates down by 4 degrees
- Visual feedback shows the change

### 2. Navigating Between Pages
**Simple Navigation Commands**:
- "Show me the kitchen"
- "Go to living room"
- "Take me to health education"
- "Show master bedroom"
- "Navigate to garage"

The app will immediately switch to the requested page.

### 3. Health Education with Images
**Complete Flow Example**:

**Step 1**: Navigate to Health Education
- Say: "Go to health education" or "Show health page"
- App switches to Health Education view

**Step 2**: Ask Health Questions
- Say: "What is blood pressure in AD?"
- The app will:
  - Display your question
  - Show the text answer
  - Display relevant medical images/diagrams
  - Keep voice active for follow-up questions

**Other Health Questions You Can Ask**:
- "How do I prevent pressure injuries?"
- "What are the signs of a UTI?"
- "How should I manage my bowel routine?"
- "What exercises can I do safely?"

### 4. Complete Interaction Example

1. **Start with Smart Home Control**:
   - You: "The kitchen is hot"
   - App: *Navigates to kitchen, shows temperature*
   - You: "Lower the temperature"
   - App: *Animates temperature reduction*

2. **Switch to Health Information**:
   - You: "Go to health education"
   - App: *Switches to Health Education page*
   - You: "What is blood pressure in AD?"
   - App: *Shows answer with medical diagrams and images*

3. **Continue Asking Questions**:
   - You: "What are the symptoms?"
   - App: *Shows related information with images*
   - You: "How can I manage it?"
   - App: *Displays management strategies with visuals*

## Important Features

### Always-On Voice Control
- The app listens continuously - no wake word needed
- No need to press buttons or say "Hey Luna"
- Just speak naturally when you need something
- Microphone animation shows when you're speaking or the assistant is responding

### Multi-Modal Responses
- Smart home commands: Voice feedback + visual changes
- Health questions: Text answers + relevant medical images
- Navigation: Instant page switching

### Context Awareness
- In smart home pages: Commands control devices
- In health education: Questions trigger medical API
- The app understands context automatically

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Speak Clearly**: Normal conversational tone works best
2. **Complete Sentences**: "What is blood pressure?" works better than just "blood pressure"
3. **Be Specific**: "Go to kitchen" or "Show kitchen page" for navigation
4. **Natural Language**: Say "The room is hot" instead of robotic commands

## Troubleshooting

**If voice commands aren't working**:
- Check the microphone icon is visible in bottom right
- Ensure you have internet connection
- Try speaking a bit louder or clearer

**If images aren't showing in health education**:
- Wait a moment for images to load
- Try asking the question again
- Check your internet connection

**If navigation seems slow**:
- The app should switch pages immediately
- If delayed, try the command again

## Privacy Note
- Voice is processed for commands only
- Health questions are kept private
- No voice recordings are stored

Enjoy your Smart Home Controller with integrated Health Education!
